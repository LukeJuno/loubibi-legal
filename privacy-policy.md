# Loubibi Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 4 October 2026**

Loubibi ("**we**", "**us**", "**our**") makes a mobile app for tracking a baby's sleep, feeding,
diaper changes, pumping, growth and milestones with your family or other caregivers. This policy
explains what information the app collects, why, and what choices you have.

If anything here is unclear, contact us at **loubibiapp@gmail.com**.

## 1. Who this policy is for

Loubibi is intended for parents, guardians and other adult caregivers who track information
*about* a baby. The app is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information
directly from a child. Information you enter about a baby (their name, birth date, and care
entries) is provided by you, the adult using the app, on the baby's behalf.

## 2. Information we collect

### Account information
When you create an account, we collect:
- Your **email address** and a hashed password (email sign-in), **or**
- The name and email address Apple or Google shares with us when you use **Sign in with Apple**
  or **Sign in with Google** (Apple lets you hide your real email behind a private relay address,
  which we support).

### Information you enter in the app
- **Baby profile:** name, date of birth, and optionally sex.
- **Care entries:** sleep, feeding, diaper, pumping and growth records, and milestones you mark as
  achieved — including timestamps, amounts, notes, and which caregiver logged each entry.
- **Family/caregiver info:** your display name, and the display names of anyone you invite to your
  family so entries can show who logged what.
- **Language preference.**

### Information collected automatically
- Basic diagnostic information needed to keep the app working (for example, that a request to our
  database succeeded or failed). We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and the
  app does not track you across other apps or websites.
- **Local notifications:** reminders (for example, "this nap has been running a while") are
  scheduled and shown entirely on your device. We don't send push notifications from a server, and
  no notification content is transmitted to us.

### Information we do not collect
We don't collect precise location, contacts, photos, or device advertising identifiers, and we
don't sell or share your information with data brokers or advertisers.

## 3. How we use information

We use the information above to:
- Create and secure your account, and let you sign in.
- Store and sync your family's entries across devices and caregivers.
- Let you invite other caregivers to your family, via a short, single-use invite code.
- Operate, maintain, and troubleshoot the app.
- Comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your data to train AI models, and we do not use it for advertising.

## 4. Who can see your data

- **Your family's caregivers.** Anyone you invite into your family (by sharing an invite code) can
  see and add to that family's baby profiles and entries. The family's main caregiver (the person who created it) can remove other caregivers from Settings.
- **Our service providers.** We use **Google Firebase** (Authentication, Cloud Firestore) to store
  account and app data, and, if you sign in that way, **Apple** and **Google** to authenticate you.
  These providers process data on our behalf under their own data-processing terms and don't use
  it for their own purposes.
- **Nobody else.** We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with
  advertisers or other third parties for their own marketing.

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of
our users or the public.

## 5. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure via Firebase. Firebase may process and store
data in the United States or other countries where Google operates data centers. By using the app,
you consent to this transfer.

## 6. How long we keep your data

We keep your account and entries for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account
from within the app, your account and personal profile are deleted from our active systems. Data held
in backups ages out on its own schedule, and we may keep limited information where we're required to
by law or for security.

What happens to shared family data depends on who you are:
- **A caregiver who joined a family:** your account is deleted, and the entries you logged stay with the
  family, shown as "Former caregiver" instead of your name.
- **The family's main caregiver:** you can delete your account once you're the only person left in the
  family. Doing so also deletes the family, its babies and all of its entries, because no one else
  would be left to see them.

## 7. Your choices and rights

- **Access and correction:** you can view and edit most of your data directly in the app.
- **Deletion:** you can delete individual entries, and you can delete your entire account, from within
  the app (Settings, then "Delete account"). See section 6 for what happens to shared family data.
- **Export:** the app offers an export of your family's data in Settings.
- **Hong Kong:** we handle personal data in line with Hong Kong's Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance.
  You can ask us for access to, or correction of, the personal data we hold about you.
- **Depending on where you live** (for example, the EU/UK under the GDPR, or certain US states),
  you may have additional rights, such as the right to obtain a copy of your data, object to
  certain processing, or lodge a complaint with a regulator. Contact us at **loubibiapp@gmail.com** to
  exercise these rights.

## 8. Children's privacy

Loubibi is a tool for adults to track information about a baby — it is not directed at children,
and we do not knowingly allow children to create accounts or use the app themselves. If you
believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

## 9. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit,
authenticated access, and database rules that restrict each family's data to that family's own
caregivers. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we can't guarantee absolute
security.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last
updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
